# A.C.O.R.N page-by-page review (2026-11-18)

Companion to `UI_MODERNIZATION_PLAN.md`. I opened every page in the sidebar and the settings hub on the live site (acorn-ext), waited for it to load, and clicked every tab, period button and filter chip on it, recording what was on the page and what it did. Numbers are measured from the page, not guessed. Pages I did **not** open: the client dashboard and audit portal (a different sign-in), My policies, Admin, Analytics, Dashboards, Marketplace, the MSSP sub-pages, the login page, and the settings pages that need a client chosen first (they are covered by the same patterns below).

## Things that are wrong on every page (fix first, they are bugs, not style)

1. **The sidebar fetches every page in advance.** One page load made over 6,000 requests: the 80 sidebar links are each prefetched, and again after each click. This is what makes the site feel slow and loads the server (the API answered `/mssp/children` in 6.6 s and `/health` in 4.5 s while it was happening). Fix: turn prefetch off for the sidebar links (`prefetch={false}`), prefetch only on hover.
2. **The client picker blocks the page.** Every Compliance page, Billing sync, Metrics pages and Client posture wait for the list of clients (`/mssp/children`, 6.6 s) before they show anything, so they sit on "Loading" for up to 10 s. Fix: show the page with the last chosen client at once and fill the picker in behind; cache the list for the session.
3. **Pages looked blank because the API itself was slow, not because they have no empty state.** While I reviewed, every API call took about 6 s (`/auth/me` 5.3 s, `/alerts` 6.6 s, a metrics call 6.3 s) because the single API process was at 100% CPU: the API log shows POSTs from CORE's feed taking 5 to 34 s each, and one process serves everyone while they run. The Metrics pages that showed only a title (Ticket resolution, Ticket volume, Pods, Staff hours, Staff metrics, Ticket updates, Customer experience, Monthly invoicing, DialPad, Labor hours, User roles, Profitability) did fill in after about 15 s. They still need a skeleton while waiting, and the API needs more than one worker (see the `api-workers` roadmap item).
4. **Sideways scrolling of the whole page at phone width** (the browser pane was at phone width for part of the first pass; checked again at 1440 px: Alerts and Identities do not scroll sideways on a desktop). At phone width SOC Insights, Identities, Alerts, Team Analytics, Honeytokens, Controls, Risk register, Vendors and Revisions scroll as a whole page. Fix on the phone layout only: wide tables inside their own horizontal scroller.
5. **Loading never ends or is silent**: Shifts, Fleet posture (all six tabs), DMARC, Vulnerabilities, Settings > Billing (all eight tabs), MFA status, Secure score, Sign-in scores, Playbooks, EASM, HubSpot, Auto scripts, Roadmap, Client mapping, Client cleanup, Retention, Announcements, Platform health, CIPP actions (shows only a header) still showed "Loading" or a dash after 3.5 s with no skeleton.
6. **Errors left on the page**: Attack Graph shows "Could not load"; Coverage Advisor showed no heading and no content after 3.5 s (not checked longer).
7. **Two menu entries open the same page**: Investigate redirects to Hunt ("Hunt at the speed of thought").
8. **Huge pages**: Settings > Guide is 170,000 characters on one page (187 tiny-text elements); What's new is 60,000; Revisions is 22,000. They need a table of contents, search and collapsing, or a split into sections.
